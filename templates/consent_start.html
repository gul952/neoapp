About this study

We are collecting information about your baby's health to help improve a computer tool that helps doctors assess the risk of infection in newborns.

Taking part is completely voluntary. Declining will not affect your baby's care in any way, and you can withdraw at any time.

Your name and contact details are stored separately from your baby's health information, and only the study team can access them.
